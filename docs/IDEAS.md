# Ideas for later

Things we've talked about building but haven't yet.

## Styles you can pick per person (Settings)

Each person chooses how Wheelhouse looks; new movies fit every style automatically, because each
style is drawn from the poster and title Wheelhouse already has (nothing extra at rip time).

- **Video rental store** (the one to do first): movies as VHS tapes on shelves with spine labels, a
  "New Releases" wall, a "Be kind, rewind" sticker on what you've watched, and a tape-tracking wobble
  when a movie starts. Could also be its own walk-around building next to the voxel theater.
- **80s neon**: glow on black, chunky chrome titles, pixelated posters (the theater already makes these).
- **Drive-in**: a night sky, rows of cars, a speaker box on the window.
- **Cinema marquee**: lit-bulb "Now showing" letter boards, a curtain that opens on Play.
- **Game Boy**: four shades of green, pixel posters, a D-pad feel (good on a TV).
- **Art house**: minimal and editorial, film-festival laurels.
- **Late-night cable**: a channel-guide grid where the library plays as channels on a schedule.
